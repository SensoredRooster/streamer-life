# Streamer Life — Locked Design Brief

Fame is a hostile market. Live is the factory. Shorts are the storefront. Owned audience is the bunker. Integrity is spendable. Burnout can beat a green graph.

Art lock: no cartoon humanoid, VTuber body, or robot character in the GUI. Live is gameplay capture plus overlay chrome and typography. Webcam is optional and defaults off. Palette: #07090C, #10161C, #1EE6C5, #0E6F62, #FF3B5C, #F5C542, #7A8894, #E8F0F4.

Always show real and display CCV separately: `ccv_display = ccv_real + ccv_bots`. Ending score excludes vanity followers and bottled viewers: `owned_audience * (integrity/100) * (1 - burnout/120) * health`.

Core loop: LIVE → CLIP → POST → COMMUNITY → MONEY/REP/HEAT → REST/LIFE. Platforms are opposing factions; payout delay, chat culture, fraud weather, funnel math, post-stream crash, and owned land are first-class systems.

Stable IDs include Q00–Q10, the Dark Market SKUs, and the GOOD/BAD/UGLY event IDs used by the prototype.
